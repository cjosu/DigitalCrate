Goes-well-with: blind pair test

Each block is one sample. Below it, partners picked by two different recommendation models, shuffled and unlabelled. Press ▶ to hear the sample and a partner together, looping and matched in loudness. Rate how well they belong together, from 1 (clash, or no reason to be together) to 5 (I'd use these together). Keys: ↑/↓ move between partners, space play/stop, 1–5 rate. About 25 blocks; partial results count too.
